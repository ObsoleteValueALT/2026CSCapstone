TOP 5 CHEESES

  1. P'tit Basque

    2. My personal all time favorite, a rich and pungent French cheese made of sheep's milk. Perfect in thin slices for a simple snack alongside aged meats or crackers!

  2. Parmesan

    3. A rather famous Italian cow's milk cheese aged for at least a year, ideal for a special treat! The best, of course, comes straight off the wheel (maybe even shredded over pasta?)

  3. Burrata

    4. Another delicious Italian treat made of cow (or rarely buffalo) milk with a soft cheese outside and a creamy inside, the perfect topper for salads or combo alongside tomatoes and other snacks!

  4. Mozzarella

    5. Similar to Burrata but with a firmer texture, mozzarella is also made from cow or buffalo milk and is often used on pizza or pasta.

  5. Cheddar

    6. A classic American staple produced from cow's milk in large quantities, reliable for grilled cheese or as a simple addition to any other sandwich!
